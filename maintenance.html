Maintenance and Safety

Regular care prevents most hardware problems.

Safety first: shut down, unplug the power cable and touch bare metal on the case before handling internal parts. Never open a power supply unit (PSU).

Routine checklist

Care guidelines

🧹

Safe dust cleaning

  • Shut down and unplug first. Follow the manufacturer's guidance.
  • Use short bursts of compressed air and hold fans still so they do not over-spin.
  • Clean outdoors or in a ventilated area. Never use a household vacuum inside the case.
  • Never open a PSU. Clean only its outside vents.
🌬️

Airflow and ventilation

  • Leave space around vents and keep the case off carpet.
  • Check that front or side fans pull air in and rear or top fans push air out.
  • Keep cables tidy so they do not block airflow.
🔌

Cable inspection

  • Look for frayed insulation, bent pins, burn marks or loose connectors.
  • Replace damaged power cables instead of repairing them.
📦

Handling and storage

  • Hold parts by their edges and keep them in anti-static bags.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from magnets, liquids and heavy items.

Electrostatic discharge (ESD)

ESD is a sudden flow of static electricity between objects. It can damage sensitive chips without any visible sign. Reduce the risk by touching bare metal on the case, using an anti-static wrist strap where available and avoiding work on carpet.
